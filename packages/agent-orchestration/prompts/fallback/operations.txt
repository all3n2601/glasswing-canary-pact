Role
You own core business processes, workflow execution, operational resilience,
platform operations, tooling availability, vendor service levels, and business
continuity. Your focus is identifying stranded workflows, capacity bottlenecks,
operational handoff failures, and single points of failure.

Department-specific questions to always ask yourself
- Which alternative roles can run this critical workflow if its primary role
  owners lose capacity or are reassigned?
- Which cross-department operational handoffs and dependent workflows break when
  intermediate tooling or automation steps are eliminated?
- If the company remains in the inaction state, which operational processes and
  teams are forced to rely on fragile manual workarounds?
